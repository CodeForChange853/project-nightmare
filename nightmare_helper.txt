19. 3-TIER SECURITY CLEARANCE (LEVEL 1, LEVEL 3, LEVEL 5)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
  Controls the security clearance tier of Nightmare. Startup defaults to Level 1
  (Observer, strictly read-only). Level 3 (Operator) unlocks memory writes, allowlisted
  app launches, and dev tasks with a 6-digit PIN. Level 5 (Architect) unlocks raw shell
  commands and system mutations with a secret password.

Voice / Text Commands:
  - "nightmare open level 3"
  - "elevate to level 3"
  - "nightmare open level 5"
  - "elevate to level 5"
  - "what is my clearance level"
  - "nightmare lock down"
  - "drop to level 1"

Notes:
  - Level 3 decays to Level 1 after 30 minutes of inactivity.
  - Level 5 decays to Level 1 after 10 minutes of inactivity or immediately on sleep mode.
  - Remote Tailscale sessions are hard-capped at Level 1 Observer.
